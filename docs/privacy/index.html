VALIZIM

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026

Valizim does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising. Your trips stay on your phone.

The short version. Everything you create in Valizim - trips, items, templates - is stored in a database inside the app on your own device. It is never uploaded, never shared, and never seen by us, because there is no server to send it to.

What we collect

Nothing. Valizim has no backend, no user accounts and no telemetry. We do not know who installed the app, how often it is opened, or what anyone packs.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your trips, checklist items and saved templates are kept in a local database file inside the app's own storage on your device.
  • Your preferences - default traveller count and trip length - are stored the same way.
  • Whether you have unlocked Pro is cached locally so the app knows your purchase status without a network connection.

All of this is removed when you delete the app. You can also erase it at any time from Settings → Delete all app data.

Notifications

If you switch on departure reminders, they are scheduled by iOS on your device. No server sends them and no notification service is involved. The app asks for notification permission only at the moment you enable a reminder, and never at launch. Declining does not affect anything else in the app.

Purchases

Valizim Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether the purchase exists. We never see your payment details, billing address or Apple Account. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Sharing

A packing list leaves your device only when you choose to share it, and only to the destination you pick in the iOS share sheet. What is shared is limited to what you can see on screen: the trip name, its length and party size, the item names, quantities and which items are ticked. No identifiers, timestamps or diagnostic information are included.

Third parties

Valizim contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no attribution or tracking SDK. It makes no network requests of its own. The only network activity that can occur is Apple's own, when the App Store is queried for the price of Valizim Pro or to restore a purchase.

Tracking

Valizim does not track you across apps or websites, and does not use the Advertising Identifier. It therefore never presents an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Children

Valizim is suitable for general audiences and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your data rights

Because no personal data ever reaches us, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or delete on your behalf. You hold the only copy, and you can erase it from within the app or by deleting the app.

Changes

If a future version of Valizim changes how data is handled, this page and the app's own Privacy screen will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top of this page will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's issue tracker or the support address listed on the App Store page.
